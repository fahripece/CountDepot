1. Log into Amazon Seller Central Developer Console and create an SP-API application.
2. Copy your LWA Client ID and Client Secret from the app credentials.
3. Complete the authorization flow to get a Refresh Token.
4. For cXML punch-out, enter your Amazon Business cXML credentials (provided by Amazon Business support).
5. For US marketplace, the Marketplace ID is ATVPDKIKX0DER.
