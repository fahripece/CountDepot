How-To Guides
Getting Started
What is CountDepot?
CountDepot is an inventory management system for tracking physical assets — IT equipment, tools, devices, retail stock — across your organization. You can check items in and out, track locations, scan barcodes, and generate reports.
How is inventory organized?
Everything follows a three-level hierarchy: Category → Product → Item. A Category is a broad type (e.g. Laptops). A Product is a specific model (e.g. ThinkPad X1 Carbon). An Item is a physical unit with its own serial number or SKU. You must create a Category and Product before you can add Items.
Where do I start after logging in?
Go to Setup → Categories to create your categories. Then go to Setup → Products to define your product models. Once products exist, you can add items from the main Inventory page using the + Add Item button.
Adding & Managing Items
How do I add a single item?
Click + Add Item in the top bar on the Inventory page. Select a product, enter the serial/SKU, cost, shelf location, and any other details. If the product has Print Label enabled, CountDepot offers a label after the item is saved.
What's the difference between serial-tracked and qty-tracked items?
Serial-tracked items are individual units with unique serial numbers (e.g. a laptop). Each one gets its own row. Qty-tracked items are counted in bulk (e.g. a box of cables) — you track how many you have, not individual units. This is set at the product level.
How do I edit an item?
Click any item row to open the detail panel on the right. Click the Edit button to change any field. Changes are saved immediately and appear in the audit log.
How do I delete items?
Open the item detail panel and click Delete at the bottom. For bulk deletes, check multiple items using the checkboxes (admin only) and use the bulk action bar. Deleted items are soft-deleted and remain in the audit log.
Checking Out & Checking In
How do I check out an item?
Open the item's detail panel and click Check Out. Enter who the item is going to (required), an optional job reference, and an expected return date. The item will show as Checked Out in red on the inventory list.
How do I check an item back in?
Open the detail panel for a checked-out item and click Check In. You can add a return note. The item returns to Available status.
How do I use the Checkout page?
The Checkout page in the nav is a scan-first workflow — scan a barcode or search for an item to quickly check it out or in without navigating the inventory table. Useful for high-volume workflows.
Where can I see all checked-out items?
Use the Status filter on the Inventory page and select Checked Out. Or go to Checked Out in the sidebar for a dedicated view.
Sites & Locations
What are Sites?
Sites represent physical locations — offices, warehouses, client sites. Each item can be assigned to a site. Admins can restrict workers to only see and manage items at their assigned site(s).
How do I view items at a specific site?
Go to Sites in the sidebar and click on a site. The site page shows only items assigned to that location, with the same filters and actions as the main inventory view.
How do I assign items to a site?
When adding or editing an item, select the site from the Site dropdown. You can also use the Assign Items button on a site page to pull in unassigned items in bulk.
How do I restrict a user to a specific site?
Go to Users & Permissions (admin only) and click the Sites button on a user's row. Assign them to one or more sites. They will only be able to see items and data for those sites.
Importing Inventory & Invoices
What file formats can I import?
The importer accepts PDF and Excel (.xlsx) files for both invoice and inventory imports.
What's the difference between Invoice Import and Inventory Import?
Invoice Import reads a vendor invoice and creates items from the line items. It auto-detects the vendor, reads the SHIP TO address to suggest a site, and matches SKUs to existing products. Inventory Import is for bulk uploading a spreadsheet of items you've already catalogued (e.g. migrating from another system).
What happens if a SKU on an invoice doesn't match any product?
Unmatched SKUs appear in a separate section after the preview. You can choose to create each one as a new item — select a category (or create a new one), and CountDepot will auto-create the product and item for you.
Where can I see past imports?
Admins can view all past imports at Reporting → Import History. It shows vendor, date, items created, and any notes.
Reports & Audit Log
What reports are available?
Financial — revenue, cost, and profit overview.
Activity — daily activity broken down by action type.
User Activity — what each user has done and when.
Checkout History — full record of every checkout/check-in cycle.
Sites Report — item counts and value broken down by location.
Import History — log of every import (admins only).
What is the Audit Log?
The Audit Log records every action taken in the system — who added, edited, deleted, checked out, or imported items, and when. It is append-only and cannot be altered. Accessible from the sidebar under Audit Log.
Low Stock & Alerts
How do I set up low stock alerts?
Go to Setup → Products, open a product, and set a Low Stock Threshold. When available inventory drops to or below that number, the product appears on the Low Stock page and a banner appears on the inventory page.
Can I get email alerts for low stock?
Yes — if your server has email (SMTP) configured, alerts are sent automatically to admin users when a product drops below its threshold. You can also set a site-specific alert email address from the Sites page.
