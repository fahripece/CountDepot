Features

Everything your team needs.
Nothing you don't.

Built for businesses who are tired of spreadsheets and overpriced enterprise software.

Asset Tracking

Track every item by serial number, SKU, or barcode. Know exactly where every asset is at all times — across locations, jobs, and teams.

Check In / Check Out

Assign items to jobs, locations, or team members. Log who took what and when — with a full audit trail and one-click return.

Barcode Scanning

Plug in any USB barcode scanner and it works instantly. Scan to check items in or out without typing a thing.

Financial Reports

Know your stock value, track what you've sold, calculate profit margins. Real numbers, not guesses.

Low Stock Alerts

Set thresholds on any item and get alerted before you run out. Stay stocked and keep operations running without interruption.

Team Permissions

Control what each team member can see and do. Staff can check items in and out without ever touching financial data.

Label Printing

Print barcoded asset labels directly from the app. Works with standard label printers and Avery sheets.

Custom Categories

Choose your industry on setup and get pre-configured categories. IT, Healthcare, Retail, Construction — all covered.

Full Audit Log

Every action is permanently recorded. See exactly who did what and when — immutable and searchable.
